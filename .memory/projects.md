## 1. Python-project1: Customer Order Analysis
* **Folder Location**: [Python-project1](file:///media/jignesh/Data/ihfc/IHFC-Leaning/Python-project1)
* **Main Script**: [customer_order_analysis.py](file:///media/jignesh/Data/ihfc/IHFC-Leaning/Python-project1/src/customer_order_analysis.py)
* **Dataset**: `customer_orders.json` (10 customers, 100 orders)
* **Key Tasks**:
  * `task1_store_orders()`: Store orders as lists, tuples, and dictionaries.
  * `task2_classify_products()`: Classify products and categories using sets.
  * `task3_analyze_orders()`: Analyze spending and classify buyers.
    * **High-value**: total spending > $100
    * **Moderate**: $50 to $100
    * **Low-value**: below $50
  * `task4_business_insights()`: Calculate total revenue, unique products, and find top 3 spenders.
  * `task5_display_data()`: Display formatted outputs of analyses.
  * `run_all()`: Runs all task routines.
